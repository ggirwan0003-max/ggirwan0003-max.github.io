TRADO Alerts

Automated email notifications for the TRADO trading platform

About TRADO Alerts

TRADO Alerts is the email notification service of TRADO, a trading-automation platform built on Salesforce. It uses the Gmail API to send short, automated emails to TRADO clients and administrators, so that everyone knows what they need to do before the market opens and how their trading day went.

What TRADO Alerts sends

Daily login reminders
Clients who have not yet connected their broker account for the day receive a reminder email each morning before market open.
Administrator summaries
TRADO administrators receive a summary showing which clients are ready to trade and which still need to connect.
Trade and system notifications
Short status messages about trades and errors generated by the TRADO Salesforce org.

How it works

  1. The TRADO administrator signs in once with Google and grants TRADO Alerts permission to send email from the administrator's Gmail account.
  2. Google returns an OAuth token, which is stored securely in Salesforce.
  3. Scheduled jobs in Salesforce use that token to send notification emails through the Gmail API.

TRADO Alerts only sends email. It does not read, search, store or analyse the contents of any Gmail mailbox.

Who can use it

TRADO Alerts is used by the TRADO administrator and serves TRADO clients. Clients do not need to sign in to anything to receive emails; this page and the privacy policy are publicly available to everyone.

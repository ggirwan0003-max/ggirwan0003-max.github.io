TRADO Alerts – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TRADO Alerts ("TRADO Alerts", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information, including information received from Google APIs. TRADO Alerts is the email notification service of the TRADO trading platform, which runs on Salesforce. See the TRADO Alerts home page for a description of the service.

1. Information we collect

InformationSourceWhy we need it
Google account email address of the administrator who connects GmailGoogle OAuth sign-inTo identify the account emails are sent from
OAuth access and refresh tokensGoogle OAuth sign-inTo send email through the Gmail API without asking the administrator to sign in every time
Client names and email addressesRecords in the TRADO Salesforce org, entered by the administratorTo address and deliver notification emails
Notification content (e.g. login status, trade status)Generated by the TRADO Salesforce orgTo compose the body of each notification email

We do not collect payment card details, government identifiers, location data, or the contents of anyone's Gmail mailbox.

2. Google user data and Gmail API scopes

TRADO Alerts requests the Gmail send permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send) only. This permission lets TRADO Alerts send email on behalf of the connected account. It does not allow TRADO Alerts to read, search, modify or delete existing emails, and TRADO Alerts never does so.

3. How we use information

  • To send daily login reminders to TRADO clients.
  • To send status summaries to TRADO administrators.
  • To send trade and system notifications generated by the TRADO Salesforce org.

We do not use any information for advertising, profiling, selling, or for training artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Limited Use disclosure

TRADO Alerts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information or Google user data. Information is shared only:

  • with Google, to deliver email through the Gmail API;
  • with Salesforce, which hosts the TRADO org where tokens and client records are stored;
  • when required by law, regulation or legal process.

6. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored in Salesforce Named Credentials / External Credentials, which encrypt secrets at rest and restrict access to authorised administrators. All communication with Google and Salesforce uses HTTPS (TLS). Access to the TRADO Salesforce org is limited to the administrator.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • OAuth tokens are kept only while TRADO Alerts is connected. They are deleted when the administrator disconnects the integration or revokes access.
  • Client contact details are kept while the person is a TRADO client and are deleted from the Salesforce org on request or when they stop being a client.
  • Sent notification emails remain in the sending account's Gmail "Sent" folder under Google's own retention controls.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can revoke TRADO Alerts' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clients can ask to stop receiving notifications, or to have their contact details deleted, by contacting the developer (see section 10).

9. Children

TRADO Alerts is not directed to anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Contact

11. Changes to this policy

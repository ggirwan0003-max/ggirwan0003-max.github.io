Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the TRADO Salesforce integration ("the app") handles Google user data.

Who uses the app

The app is a private tool used only by its developer with the developer's own Google account. It is not available to the public.

Google data accessed

The app requests permission to send email through the Gmail API on behalf of the signed-in account. It uses this permission only to send automated notification emails from a Salesforce org that the developer owns.

How data is used and stored

  • The app does not read, download, index or analyse email content.
  • OAuth tokens are stored securely in Salesforce (Named / External Credentials) and are used only to send these notifications.
  • Recipient email addresses come from records in the developer's own Salesforce org.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
